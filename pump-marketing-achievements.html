01 · Team-oriented focus

It started with the team

Team photo goes here
1

Recognised and developed individual potential

We looked at each person's strengths before assigning territories and roles. People who showed a knack for technical selling, dealer relations or field activation were given work that used it, and room to grow further. Regular one-to-one conversations helped us spot hidden ability early, set personal goals and turn quiet performers into confident owners of their markets.

2

Focused training and capability building

Training was built around what the market asked of us. Product sessions covered pump selection, applications and common site problems, while selling skills were practised on real dealer situations. New joiners learned alongside experienced colleagues in the field. The result was a team able to answer technical questions at the counter and recommend the right pump with confidence.

3

Knowledge sharing for collective growth

What one person learned became everyone's advantage. Wins, lost orders and competitor moves were shared openly in our reviews and group chats, so a good idea in one territory was tried in the next within days. Senior members passed on their experience freely, and the whole team improved faster than any individual could have managed working alone.

4

Built team spirit and bonding

We made time to know each other beyond targets. Team meets, shared celebrations of milestones and simple gestures of appreciation built trust across locations. When someone faced a difficult month, colleagues stepped in with support rather than comparison. That bond showed up in the market as one team speaking with one voice to every dealer and customer.

5

Enhanced cross-functional collaboration

Sales, marketing, product and service stopped working in separate lanes. Joint market visits, shared planning and quick calls between functions meant dealer issues were solved in days, not weeks. Marketing campaigns reflected what sales heard on the ground, and service feedback reached the product team directly. Customers experienced one company, not a chain of departments passing things along.

6

Maintained harmony and positive rhythm

A steady working rhythm kept the team calm and focused through busy seasons. Clear priorities, predictable reviews and fair distribution of work reduced friction and last-minute pressure. Disagreements were discussed openly and settled early. By keeping the mood positive and the pace consistent, we sustained effort through the whole year instead of peaking and fading.

02 · Segment-oriented focus

We went segment by segment

1

Identified and targeted key dealers

We mapped every market to find the dealers who truly influence pump sales in each segment. Rather than spreading effort thinly, we concentrated visits, support and schemes on these key counters. Stronger relationships with the right partners gave us better display, faster feedback from the field and a dependable base to launch each new product range.

2

Conducted live demos

Seeing a pump perform convinces faster than any brochure. We ran live demonstrations at dealer counters, farms and project sites, showing discharge, head and energy use under real conditions. Customers and mechanics could compare results for themselves and ask questions on the spot. Those sessions turned doubt into trial, and trial into repeat orders.

3

Market survey and channel expansion

Field surveys showed us where demand existed and where we were absent. We studied segment needs, competitor presence and price points town by town, then used the findings to appoint new dealers and reach uncovered areas. Decisions were based on what we saw in the market, so every new outlet opened with a clear reason behind it.

4

Customised drillers' meetings

Borewell drillers often decide which pump a customer buys. We organised meetings designed specifically for them, covering correct pump selection for depth and yield, installation practice and common failures. Listening to their site experience improved our own recommendations. The drillers left as informed partners who were confident to suggest our pumps to their customers.

5

Showcased our core strength

In every segment we led with what we do best. Product quality, proven reliability and dependable after-sales support were presented with evidence from real installations instead of general claims. Keeping one consistent message across dealers, demos and meetings helped customers remember us for the right reasons and gave our team a confident story to tell.

6

Promotional schemes and digital content

Targeted schemes gave dealers and influencers a timely reason to choose us, tied to season and segment rather than blanket discounts. Alongside them we produced short videos, product explainers and shareable posts for phones and social groups. The combination kept our brand visible between visits and carried the same message to far more people.

03 · Expanding business channels

We opened more routes to market

1

Engaged key channels

We strengthened ties with the channel partners who move the most volume. Regular visits, joint planning and prompt support on stock and claims kept them committed to our range. With our key channels fully engaged, new products reached the market faster and our presence at the counter became consistent.

2

Leveraged the project team

Our project team opened doors that the trade alone could not. By working with builders, consultants and contractors from the design stage, they got our pumps specified for apartments, commercial buildings and infrastructure work. Each project win also created a reference site that helped the next conversation.

3

Targeted outside our DMAs

We looked beyond our established market areas for growth. Towns and pockets where we had little presence were studied, visited and assigned clear responsibility. Entering these areas with the right dealer and the right range brought us customers who had never been offered our pumps before.

4

Successful installations

Every pump that performed well on site became proof for the next buyer. We made sure installations were done correctly, followed up after commissioning and recorded results. Satisfied users and their installers recommended us to others, turning good work in the field into a steady source of enquiries.

5

Appointed sanitary counters

Many home buyers choose a pump where they buy their bathroom fittings. We appointed sanitaryware counters as outlets, giving them display units, simple selection guidance and reliable supply. This placed our domestic range in front of customers at the moment they were planning their water system.

6

Developed through RSS counters

RSS counters gave us another route into the market. We supported them with the right product mix, training for counter staff and visible branding. As their confidence in our range grew, they became active sellers and extended our reach into customer groups we were not meeting before.

7

Focused on department sales

We gave dedicated attention to government and institutional departments. Understanding their specifications, tender schedules and approval steps let us respond accurately and on time. Reliable supply and service after the order built credibility, and that record made us a trusted name for later requirements.
